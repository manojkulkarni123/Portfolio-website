- obsession is the only way to get what you want. balance and growth cannot co-exist
- the life of waiting, planning and permission-seeking is no life at all
- to embrace uncertainty is to be human
- the three biggest decisions you'll ever make are where you live, who you're with, and what you do (naval)
- things are not difficult to make; what is difficult is putting ourselves in the state of mind to make them (brâncuși)
- a man with no principles doesn't know when to say yes or no
- if you want to make the wrong decision, ask everyone
- decide on one thing, start immediately, don't stop until it's done
- deadlines are the only way to feel the weight of time
- you can't do everything, but you can do the few things that matter most
- playful seriousness: take the game seriously, never yourself
- walking fixes almost everything
- writing is thinking in action
- memento mori
