- belief comes first. it decides how good of a life you will lead
- obsession rules. balance and growth cannot co-exist
- the life of waiting, of planning, of permission seeking is no life at all
- to embrace uncertainty is to be human
- you become the company you keep, so choose it consciously
- live with principles and write them down. a man with no principles doesn't know how to say no or yes
- whenever i find myself on the side of the majority, i pause and reflect
- accepting the truth is the fastest way to progress. lying to yourself is the fastest way down
- if you want to make the wrong decision, ask everyone
- decide on one thing, start immediately, keep going until it's done. repeat
- deadlines are the only way to feel the weight of time
- you can't do everything, but you can do the few things that matter most
- philosophy is a weapon to evolve, not just a tool to diagnose what's wrong
- playful seriousness is the attitude of life
- leverage is king
- walking fixes almost everything
- writing is a superpower. it drags out what's floating in your unconscious mind
- memento mori

more on the way..
